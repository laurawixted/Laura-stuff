About

I'm a Staff Content Designer at Skylight (opens in new tab), where I structure design system content and build content for government digital services, so that developers, AI tools, and the people who use these services can all rely on the same guidance.

Over 20+ years in content design and technical writing, I’ve built content frameworks and style guides across industries, from DB2 tools and cybersecurity to ed tech, AI products, and civic tech. I co-founded Pearson’s design system team, redesigned Sage’s global content style guide, and led content design for one of Sage’s first AI products.

I care about content that’s clear, concise, and accessible to everyone, and about documentation that’s easy to find, trust, and apply. I like working with cross-functional teams to solve complex problems, and I enjoy mentoring other content designers.

I also lead the Boston UX Content meetups, a community for content designers to share ideas and learn from each other. Boston UX Content meetups (opens in new tab),

When I’m not designing content, I enjoy hiking, reading, and exploring new places. I’m always learning, and I love keeping up with how content design is evolving.
